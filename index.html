_Last updated: 2026-07-12_ Relationship Tracker ("the app") is developed by Kozo Technology. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it's used. ## Data you enter into the app Everything you enter - people, relationship types, interaction history, notes, birthdays, and photos - is stored only on your device, in a local database. It is never sent to us or to any server we control. We have no way to see it. The only way this data leaves your device is if you choose to use the app's Export backup feature, which creates a file you explicitly save or share yourself (e.g. to your own cloud storage or email). We don't receive a copy. ## Advertising The app shows ads served by Google AdMob to keep the app free. AdMob and its partners may collect device identifiers, approximate location (derived from IP address), and ad interaction data to show and measure ads, and depending on your consent choice, to personalize them. This collection is governed by Google's own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy Where required (including in the EEA and UK), the app shows a consent form before requesting personalized ads, via Google's User Messaging Platform. ## Permissions - Notifications: used only to remind you about reminders you've set inside the app. Not used for any other purpose. - Photos/Camera: used only when you choose to add or change a person's photo. Photos are stored locally in the same way as other app data. - Biometric unlock: if you enable it, your device's fingerprint/face data never leaves your device or reaches this app - it's handled entirely by the operating system, which just tells the app "yes" or "no." ## Data deletion Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data. Removing a person inside the app archives them (their interaction history is kept so your stats stay accurate); there is no separate "delete my data" request to make since we never had a copy in the first place. ## Children The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children. ## Changes to this policy If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and, where required, we'll notify you via the app or store listing. ## Contact Questions about this policy: kozo.technology@gmail.com
