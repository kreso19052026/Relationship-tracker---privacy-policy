Privacy Policy for Relationship Tracker

Last updated: 2026-10-03

Relationship Tracker ("the app") is developed by Kozo Technology. This policy explains what data the app collects and how it's used.

Data you enter into the app

Everything you enter — people, relationship types, interaction history, notes, birthdays, places, and photos — is stored only on your device, in a local database. It is never sent to us or to any server we control. We have no way to see it.

Your data leaves your device only in these cases:

  • You use the app's Export backup feature, which creates a file you explicitly save or share yourself (e.g. to your own cloud storage or email). We don't receive a copy.
  • You use the online services described below.

Online services

  • Place search and maps (OpenStreetMap): when you search for a place while logging an interaction, the text you type is sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service to find matching places. Map tiles are downloaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers whenever a map is shown. These requests include your IP address, as any internet request does. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy: https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy
  • Fonts (Google Fonts): the app downloads its fonts from Google Fonts the first time they're needed, which shares your IP address with Google. See https://policies.google.com/privacy

Advertising and your consent

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob to keep the app free. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and operating-system information, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. See how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

European Economic Area, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the app asks for your consent through Google's certified consent management platform. Personalised ads are shown only if you consent; otherwise you may see limited, non-personalised ads.

United States: if you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California), you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for targeted advertising.

Change your choice at any time: open the app's Settings → Privacy options.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used only to remind you about reminders you've set inside the app. Not used for any other purpose.
  • Photos/Camera: used only when you choose to add or change a person's photo. Photos are stored locally in the same way as other app data.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data. Removing a person inside the app archives them (their interaction history is kept so your stats stay accurate); there is no separate "delete my data" request to make since we never had a copy in the first place.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and, where required, we'll notify you via the app or store listing.
